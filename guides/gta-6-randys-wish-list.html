Tips

  • Watch the clock. Cars like the Pegassi Toros, Ascot Paragon, Pfister Growler and Bravado Banshee are tied to a time of day. Come back at the right hour if they're not there.
  • Capri is the hotspot. Several cars spawn on the driveways of the wealthy homes in Capri, but the exact house can vary – drive the streets or reload if you don't see them.
