Tips

  • Go for the smaller sets first if you're after the trophy – the Backyard Wrestling set has just 12 cards and Delights Dancers has 18.
  • The sports sets are grouped by city – Vice City, Liberty City and Los Santos – which makes them easy to tell apart.
  • Tick each set off in the tracker when it's done so you know exactly where you stand.
