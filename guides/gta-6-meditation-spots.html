Suggested route

  1. Chapter 1: do Jason's House on Key Lento before the chapter ends.
  2. Mariana County: Smooth Sand on the sandbar off Capri.
  3. Kelly County: Swamp (Buckskin Swamp) and Worn Stone (Camp Moonflower).
  4. Lummox County: Water Wheel (south Moreau) and Waterfall (northwest of Tallaloko).
  5. Leonard County and Manifest: Seaside by Waning Sands and Red Rocks by the rail tracks.
  6. Mount Kalaga: Jutting Cliff, then finish at the summit with Ascent once the other 17 are done.
