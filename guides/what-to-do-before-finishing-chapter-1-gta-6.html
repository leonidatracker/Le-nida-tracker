Do these before Chapter 1 ends

These are the only things we've confirmed you can lose in Chapter 1:

{{table:activities:Chapter 1 only}}
  • Jason's House meditation is one of the {{count:activities:^Meditation:}} meditation spots. Positive Mental Attitude asks for every spot, so do it while you can – see the meditation spots guide.
  • Shirley's missions stop being available once Tourist Trap starts. They aren't needed for any trophy, so only do them if you want to see everything.

Grab these before Chapter 2 ends

Two trading cards are in parts of the Starlet Motel in Gellhorn that get shut off after Chapter 2:

{{table:collectibles:missable|can't be reached after}}

You don't need them for 100% (it only asks for 15 of the 18 card sets), but you do need them to finish the Los Santos Corkers and Delights Dancers sets. See all trading card locations.

Everything else can wait

Apart from the items above, everything on the 100% checklist can still be done after the story in free roam. A good rhythm is to follow the story, pick up side activities as they appear, and leave the big clean-up for after the credits.

The tracker flags every missable item in red at the top of the checklist, so you'll see them as soon as you open it.
