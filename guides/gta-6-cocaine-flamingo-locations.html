How Cocaine Flamingos work

  • Spot the beak. Cocaine Flamingos are pink lawn flamingos with a black-and-white beak. The ones with a black-and-yellow beak are just decoration.
  • Shoot it. Destroying the flamingo counts it and drops a cocaine package. Selling the package to a fence is optional.
  • Track it in the game under Progress › Collections › Exploration › Cocaine Flamingos once you've found your first.
  • You need 100 of the 125 for the 100% checklist, and 50 unlock the Inflatable Flamingo cheat – see the cheats list.

Route tips

  • Flamingos tend to come in clusters on lawns, docks and hotel grounds, so look around once you've found one.
  • Since you only need 100, don't burn time on a hard-to-reach one – move on and come back later if you're short.
  • Tick each one off in the tracker as you go; the map shows which areas you've cleared.
