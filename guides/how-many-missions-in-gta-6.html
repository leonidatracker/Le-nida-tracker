What isn't counted

The number above is story missions only. On top of those there are:

  • Strangers & Freaks – {{count:activities:^Strangers & Freaks:}} optional character strands, most with several missions each.
  • Robberies and jobs – bank jobs, Smash 'n' Grabs, stick-ups and gang operations.
  • Activities – races, drone time trials, stunt jumps, hunting, fishing, bowling and more.

If you're going for 100%, the side content takes far longer than the story itself.
