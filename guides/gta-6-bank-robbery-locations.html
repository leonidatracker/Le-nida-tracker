Two kinds of bank job

  • Small banks ({{count:activities:^Bank job:}}) – you hit the till and the safe. Payouts are modest, from around $5k up to about $45k at the best ones.
  • Banks with a vault ({{count:activities:^Bank vault:}}) – bigger jobs with a much bigger reward, roughly $65k to $205k depending on the bank.

They don't all open at once. Small banks become available after the Chapter 3 mission Credit Check; the vaults only open after you play the optional Chapter 4 mission That's Where The Money Is…

Where the bank vaults are

All the vaults are in and around Vice City – half of them in Ocean Beach – so you can plan a tight route once they unlock.

You only need 5 of the {{count:activities:^Bank vault:}} vaults, so you can skip the ones that give you the most trouble. Don't confuse it with Break the Bank – that one comes from a story heist, not these bank jobs.

Tips

  • Biggest payouts: the Lombank vault in Downtown Vice City and the main Lombank vault in Ocean Beach pay the most, at roughly $135k–$205k each (Ocean Beach's second Lombank vault pays less).
  • Best small banks: Lombank in Bayside, the small Lombank in Ocean Beach and Druthers State Bank in Leonard County top out around $45k.
  • Ocean Beach is the hotspot – it has {{count:activities:^Bank (job|vault):.*Ocean Beach}} of the {{count:activities:^Bank (job|vault):}} banks between small banks and vaults.
  • Bank jobs sit alongside Smash 'n' Grabs, stick-ups and payday loan robberies – see the side activities list and tick each bank off in the tracker as you go.
