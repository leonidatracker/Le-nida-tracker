The roadmap in short

  1. Play through the story. {{count:trophies:finish (Chapter|the .*mission|The Cartel|Operation)}} trophies come from story missions and chapters, and many side activities unlock as you progress.
  2. Pick up Strangers & Freaks as they appear. Several trophies need specific characters' final missions, and Strangers & Freaks needs eight strands finished.
  3. Grab the one-off trophies – most are quick once you know where to go.
  4. Finish the 100% clean-up for A Long Road. This is the biggest job, so use the tracker to see what's left.

Do this in Chapter 1: meditate at Jason's House on Key Lento. It's only available during Chapter 1, and Positive Mental Attitude asks for every meditation spot. See the meditation spots guide.
