Money and time

1. Don't walk around with your cash

Whatever cash you're carrying is gone if you die or get arrested. Until you unlock a bank account in the mission Buck Me, hide your money under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom. Once the bank is open, deposit regularly.

2. Keep an eye on the clock

Days and nights last much longer than in older GTA games. Press Up on the D-pad to see the time on your phone. Some shops shut at night, and some things only happen at certain hours – several of Randy's Wish List cars, for example. To skip the night, sleep at Jason's house.

Reading the world

3. Trust the minimap

Walk near the back door of a shop or house and the minimap shows whether there's more space behind it. Hidden rooms and staircases show up the same way when you're close – they're often where the valuables are.

4. Darker blue means you can go in

On the world map, darker blue buildings have interiors you can enter; lighter blue ones don't. Up close, the minimap marks the way in with a white line.

5. Use Focus before you open a door

With Focus on, doors you can safely go through glow white and alarmed doors glow red. Opening a red one brings attention you probably don't want.

Staying out of trouble

6. Change your look after a crime

Witnesses, alarms and cameras all feed your Criminal Profile, and the police remember what you were wearing and driving. After a heavy job, swap your outfit and your car.

7. Unlock the mechanic early

After the mission Just a Scratch you can call a mechanic to fix your car from almost anywhere. It costs money, but it's often cheaper than losing a chase.

Things that pay off later

8. Don't rush out of Chapter 1

Two things disappear once Chapter 1 is over: the meditation spot at Jason's House on Key Lento – Positive Mental Attitude asks for every meditation spot, so do it while you can (see the meditation spots) – and Shirley's two Strangers & Freaks missions, which aren't needed for any trophy.

9. Start the cheat challenges early

Cheats only work after the story, but you can work towards them from the start. 100 workouts unlock Body Armor, and shooting-range medals unlock Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload. The cheats list shows every challenge.

10. Play as a pair

Plenty of trophies and cheats need Jason and Lucia together – escaping a 4-star wanted level as a pair (Hot Together), holding up shops back to back (Retail Therapy), and cheats like Dollhouse and Faded Glory. Do partner activities as you go rather than grinding them at the end.

11. Save before you try a cheat

Using a cheat turns off saving, trophies and achievements for that session. Save first, have your fun, then reload. Money from the $250,000 cheat won't carry over.

12. You don't need everything for 100%

Some collections only need part of the total – 200 of the 277 vehicle scans, for instance. The 100% checklist lists them.

A good way to play it

  1. Play the story at your own pace and pick up Strangers & Freaks characters as they appear.
  2. Clear the Chapter 1 one-offs before you finish the chapter.
  3. Chip away at workouts, shooting ranges and partner activities in between missions – they feed trophies and cheats.
  4. After the credits, switch to clean-up. Use the tracker to see what's left for 100% and the Platinum, and turn on the cheats you've earned for fun.
